## Verdict
By the pre-registered bar the answer is **not "reprices within seconds"**: criterion 2 passes (activity in the first 5 minutes is ~2.3x the placebo, +8.5 fills per pair, CI [4.3, 13.9], robust to clock-time matching and to dropping pinned markets), but criterion 1 fails clearly — the median time to a first >=2-pt move over moved pairs is 682 s (CI 531-900), versus 1,210 s for placebo. So Truth Social posts do move Polymarket and the reaction starts quickly (median first fill 89 s vs 108 s placebo, so the first fill is not much faster than baseline in thin markets), but across keyword-matched markets the typical price displacement arrives over ~10 minutes, not seconds; only 17 % of pairs ever move >=2 pts within an hour (13 % placebo). The post-hoc split suggests a bimodal picture — a few flagship markets (Iran ceasefire, Maduro, Fed chair) reprice in 13-33 s as with scheduled releases, while the majority of loosely-related markets do not move at all — which fits "no systematic lag in the markets that matter, slow/no reaction elsewhere" better than "a measurable lag to sell". This is a market-level lag in thin, loosely related contracts, which is not evidence of an exploitable edge: there are no quotes in this archive, medians are conditional on a fill happening, and fills are timestamped at on-chain settlement (+ a few seconds).

## Caveats / unverified
- Keyword relevance is crude: 100 % of events got >=1 active market (slug terms like `fed`, `china`, `bps` are broad), so "relevance rate" says little about true topical relevance; many pairs are only loosely related. Pass criterion 1 is therefore diluted by unrelated markets, and no second labeller or LLM mapping was used.
- Events 2025-01-20..2025-10-21 are outside the 400-event cap; results are for Oct 2025 - Apr 2026 only.
- Event and placebo windows differ in news-day activity; the clock-time-matched placebo changes nothing, but post-day effects (a post inside a day with other news) are not modelled.
- Direction of the move is not scored (|dp| only).

# pmflow

Does Polymarket react to news slowly enough to trade on?

- Measured how fast prices move after CPI/Fed releases, Truth Social posts and headlines.
- Tested whether an AI model reading a post, or related markets lagging each other, gives an edge.
- Records live Polymarket quotes plus news feeds.
- Finding: liquid markets reprice within seconds. The small edges that exist are about the size of the trading fee.
